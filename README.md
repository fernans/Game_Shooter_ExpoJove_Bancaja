# Game_Shooter_ExpoJove_Bancaja

Flash (2006/2007) shooting-gallery game built for the Bancaja ExpoJove youth
fair, by Mediterrania Multimedia. The player moves a crosshair with a joystick
and shoots at moving enemy ships; the game reads its behaviour from
`disparos1.ini` (title, round time, crosshair colour). Full graphic/audio
material of the era is included: enemy ship design files, a 37-frame explosion
animation, movie soundtracks and the Flash sources. The joystick control is
provided by the third-party freeware tool **Joystick Mouse** by Michael P.
Calligaro (© 1998), kept in `joymouse/` together with its original readme.

> **This project is shared ONLY for educational purposes. Its use, direct or derived, for professional or economic purposes is not permitted.**

For security/integrity reasons, no part of the project needed to be edited or removed: everything is kept as it was left in 2006/2007, including the third-party attributions.

## Requirements / How to run

- **Windows** with a Flash Player 8 (or later) runtime and a joystick:
  1. Run `joymouse/joymouse.exe` (translates joystick movement to mouse cursor).
  2. Open `disparos1.swf` in the Flash Player/standalone projector or in a
     browser with the Flash plug-in.
- **Sources**: open `disparos1.fla` (Flash 8 or CS era) to edit the game.
  `disparos1.ini` configures title, round time and crosshair colour.
- `referencia.fla` and `Copia de disparos1.fla` are working/reference copies
  kept as delivered. `material/` holds the PSD drawings, the explosion frames,
  the music/audio effects and the original zips the material was unpacked from.

## Third-party tool

# Game_Shooter_ExpoJove_Bancaja

Juego Flash (2006/2007) tipo galería de tiro construido para la feria juvenil
ExpoJove de Bancaja, por Mediterrania Multimedia. El jugador mueve una mira
con un joystick y dispara a naves enemigas en movimiento; el juego lee su
comportamiento de `disparos1.ini` (título, tiempo de ronda, color de la mira).
Se incluye el material gráfico/sonoro completo de la época: diseños de naves
enemigas, una animación de explosión de 37 frames, bandas sonoras de películas
y las fuentes Flash. El control por joystick lo aporta la herramienta freeware
de terceros **Joystick Mouse de Michael P. Calligaro (© 1998)**, conservada en
`joymouse/` junto a su readme original.

## Requisitos / Cómo ejecutar

- **Windows** con runtime Flash Player 8 (o superior) y joystick:
  1. Ejecutar `joymouse/joymouse.exe` (traduce el movimiento del joystick al
     cursor del ratón).
  2. Abrir `disparos1.swf` en el flash player standalone/proyector o en un
     navegador con el plugin de Flash.
- **Fuentes**: abrir `disparos1.fla` (Flash 8 o era CS) para editar el juego.
